Listora is a beautiful, free audiobook player for the public-domain classics of LibriVox, served from the Internet Archive. No subscriptions, no ads, no account — just thousands of timeless books, read aloud by volunteers, ready whenever you are.

Browse the most-downloaded and newly-added titles, search by title or author, and tap any author to explore everything they've narrated. Found something you love? Add it to Favourites and it's always one tap away.

An immersive, distraction-free player puts the story first: a full-screen now-playing view with artwork, a smooth scrubber, skip back 10s / forward 30s, adjustable playback speed, and a sleep timer for bedtime listening. Lock-screen and notification controls keep you in charge without unlocking your phone, and Listora remembers exactly where you left off in every book.

Going offline? Download a single chapter or a whole book for listening on the plane, the trail, or the commute — downloaded chapters play straight from your device, and your Library keeps Favourites, Continue listening, and Downloaded neatly in one place.

FEATURES
• Thousands of free, public-domain audiobooks (LibriVox / Internet Archive)
• Search by title or author, plus "More by this author"
• Favourites and a personal Library
• Resume playback exactly where you stopped
• Download for offline listening (per chapter or whole book)
• Background playback with lock-screen & notification controls
• Adjustable speed (0.75×–2×) and a sleep timer
• Immersive light and dark themes

No sign-up. No cost. Just great books, beautifully read.

All audio is in the public domain, provided by LibriVox volunteers via the Internet Archive. Listora is an independent player and is not affiliated with LibriVox or the Internet Archive.
